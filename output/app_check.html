Campus Customs: App Check

1. Chatbot checks the inventory level for a size

On the Baseball Left Chest Crewneck page, the shopper asks "do you have this in XS?" without naming the product. Handsome Dan knows which item they mean from the page context, looks up live stock, and correctly says XS is out of stock and lists the sizes that are available (S, M, L, XXL), matching the database and the size boxes on the page.

2. Dynamic page update from the chatbot

Asking "yall have hoodies?" makes the agent search the catalogue and return structured product matches, which the website renders as a "From your chat: Yale hoodies" panel of product cards on the page itself. The reply's totals come from the database: 27 hoodies, $45.00 to $88.00, with 15 more beyond the 12 shown.

3. Sorting products by price

The Products page has a Sort by control with Name (A to Z), Price: Low to High, and Price: High to Low. Choosing Low to High reorders all 102 products from the $32.00 T-shirts up to the $98.00 jackets.
